Privacy and security

Spatial data is personal data

Collect less

Eye tracking, body movement, voice, face geometry and room maps are treated as sensitive. We collect only what an experience needs, with consent where required.

Keep it apart

Tenant-isolated data, encrypted assets, short-lived scoped tokens and phishing-resistant sign-in for administrators.

Safe shared spaces

Moderation tools and protections against harassment, impersonation and account takeover in shared virtual spaces. Likeness and voice are used only with express permission.
